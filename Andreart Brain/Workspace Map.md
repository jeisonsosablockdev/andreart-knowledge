# Marketing Workspace Map

This vault is the persistent content and knowledge layer of the project. Final marketing deliverables and campaign notes are stored here as structured Markdown.

## Folder & Workflow Map

- `00 Inbox`: raw ideas, temporary drafts, active task tracking JSON sessions (`active-task-session.json`)
- `01 Brand Context`: persistent product and brand context (`product-marketing-context.md`) — **[W1: Brand Strategy]**
- `02 Strategy & Research`: research, positioning, content strategy, launch strategy, pricing — **[W1 & W5]**
- `03 Website & Copy`: website copy, landing pages, editing, lead magnets — **[W2: Landing Pages & Copy]**
- `04 Email & Lifecycle`: cold email and email sequence work — **[W4: Email Lifecycle]**
- `05 SEO & Discoverability`: SEO audits, AI SEO, ASO, programmatic SEO, schema, site architecture — **[W3: SEO & AI SEO]**
- `06 CRO & Funnel`: page CRO, signup, onboarding, form, popup, paywall, A/B testing — **[W6: CRO & Experimentos]**
- `07 Paid, Social & Community`: paid ads, ad creative, social content, community marketing — **[W5: Demand Gen & Social]**
- `08 Analytics & Measurement`: tracking plans, KPI scorecards and measurement artifacts — **[W8: Analytics & Metrics]**
- `09 Retention & Growth`: churn prevention, referral programs, free tool strategy — **[W7: Sales & Retention]**
- `10 RevOps & Sales`: revops, sales battlecards, objection handling and enablement assets — **[W7: Sales & Retention]**

## Working Rules

- Use this vault for readable, structured Markdown outputs using Obsidian Properties (frontmatter) and callouts
- Keep skills, system docs, and adaptation logic in `../MarketingAgentCore/`
- Always verify or consult `[[01 Brand Context/product-marketing-context.md]]` before creating new deliverables
